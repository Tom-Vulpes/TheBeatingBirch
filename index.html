What holds the tree up

A hopeful, speculative answer to what technology and nature could become together

The Beating Birch isn't a warning story. It's a proposal — a civilisation that grew in cooperation with a living system rather than in spite of one, told through the specific discipline of giving something back before you take.

01

Reciprocity as infrastructure

Sonic offering isn't decoration in this world — it's how permission, gratitude and attention actually get exchanged with the tree.

02

Technology as tending

Tools and instruments exist to listen and respond to the Birch, not to extract from it — built objects as acts of care.

03

A future worth wanting

Hope here isn't naive optimism — it's a specific, workable picture of coexistence you can walk through, branch by branch.
